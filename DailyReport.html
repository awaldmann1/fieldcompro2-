How It Works
1

AS Opens This Link

Each AS taps a URL at end of day — no login, no app to install. Opens in Safari on iPhone or iPad.

2

Voice or Tap

Tap 🎤 to dictate hands-free in English or Spanish. Dropdowns stay click-only for Procore data integrity.

3

Procore Log Builds Live

The form data formats into a Procore daily log entry in real time — no re-typing, no reformatting.

4

Super Reviews & Approves

You see all AS reports in one view. Review, approve, and Procore updates automatically.

5

Dead Zones? No Problem

Reports queue offline and auto-send when signal returns. Nothing gets lost in the field.

🎤 Voice tip: Tap the mic, wait a beat, speak naturally. Tap again to stop. Requires Safari on iOS 15+ or Chrome on Android.
